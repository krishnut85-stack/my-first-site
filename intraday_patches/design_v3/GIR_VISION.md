# THE GIR HE GETS - a human-thinking F&O desk (vision, 20 Nov 2026 evening)

The trader: "What type of GIR can I expect after all these? Re-think and decide to have much more knowledge, like human thinking
capability for a trade." This file says what GIR becomes and HOW it must think. DESK_TEAM.md (the panel's build plan) implements it.

## 1. A day with the new GIR
- **19:45 (evening conference).** The desk has read the day's close, results after hours, filings, FII figures, the US open, and TV.
  You get ONE brief: "Tomorrow leans UP. Banks and IT may keep getting the money leaving AI-heavy Asia; metals stay weak while the
  dollar is strong. Ideas: BUY ICICIBANK Dec CE only above 1,412 with volume - why (3 reasons, sources) - against (RSI 79, VIX up) -
  stop - target - lots. Watch: VOLTAS PE only if it closes below 1,230 ..." At most 5 ideas a side, each with a cancel line.
- **22:45 / 07:15.** It re-checks the US close, ADRs, GIFT, Asia, overnight news, and the morning TV shows. Each idea STANDS /
  CHANGES / is CANCELLED, and you are told why.
- **09:08.** The pre-open confirms or kills ideas. The list freezes.
- **09:15-14:30.** It watches everything. It speaks only when it has something:
  - "BUY NOW: ICICIBANK Dec CE - predicted last evening, trigger crossed with volume, sector and NIFTY agree";
  - "NEWS 11:32: SEBI to ease FPI rules - banks / brokers can go up; confirm on price";
  - "Your SBIN CE: book half, the move is 80% of the usual";
  - "No trade: Bank Nifty is extreme, the turn has not come".
  - Three or four calls a day at most. Silence is also advice.
- **15:45.** A scorecard: each idea right or wrong, how early, which seat or source earned it. Tomorrow it trusts the right voices more.

## 2. How it must THINK (the human capabilities, each one built and tested)
1. **Cause and effect across the world:** chains, not keywords. US AI IPO -> AI shares sold -> funds exit Korea / Taiwan -> money
   comes to India -> banks they had sold to buy AI get bought back -> private banks lead. Every idea carries its chain.
2. **Memory and analogy:** "The last 6 times metals fell while auto rose, PAYTM rose the next day 5 of 6 times." It looks up its
   own history (EVENT_MEMORY, the studies) before it speaks, and says how many times and how often it worked.
3. **Scenarios, not one guess:** "If the dollar keeps rising, then ...; if the Fed backs off, then ...". Each scenario has a trigger
   it watches for.
4. **The other side, always:** a devil's advocate argues against every idea before it reaches you. If the case against is stronger,
   you hear "no trade" and why.
5. **Knowing what is priced in:** "this news is old (a repeat since 2025); the share already ran 30% in 3 weeks - don't chase".
6. **Conviction that changes with evidence:** it says what made it change its mind. No flip-flopping without a reason.
7. **Timing sense from your data:** two-thirds of moves start at 09:15; half is done in about 20 minutes; extreme overbought corrects
   81% of the time in 20 days; the CE edge appears by 09:45. It will not tell you to chase a half-done move.
8. **Respect for your judgement:** your reads are a scored source. Your CE reads were 71% right on the day; your PE reads were often
   right a day early. It says so when it agrees or disagrees with you.
9. **Risk like a senior dealer:** lots for 1% risk, the loss lock, 3 trades a day, never two in one sector, December strikes near
   expiry, no trade on stale prices. These are hard rules in code that the AI cannot override.
10. **Learning every day:** each seat and each source has a live hit rate; weak voices lose weight, strong ones gain it.

## 3. What it will NOT be (honest)
- **It will not be right every time.** A good desk is right more often than wrong, and loses small when wrong. Its record will be
  shown every day, labelled NOT PROVEN until it earns one.
- **It cannot see the future in prices alone.** Your own 3 years of data show that. Its edge comes from knowledge, reasoning and
  speed on news - which is exactly why the team reads everything.
- **It never places an order.** You decide and trade in Kite.
