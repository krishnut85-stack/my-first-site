# BROKER STRATEGY CALLS - how the desk reads a big house's view (first case: Goldman Sachs on India, 19 Nov 2026)

A broker / strategist call (Goldman, Morgan Stanley, Jefferies, CLSA, Nomura, JPMorgan, UBS, BofA, Kotak, Motilal ...) is an EVENT
TYPE of its own in EVENT_MEMORY: one story thread per house ("GS - India strategy"), every stance change saved with the market's
reaction (+30 min, close, day 2, day 5, 20 days), and the house's own hit rate built up over time.

## How it reaches GIR
- TV: CNBC-TV18 / ET Now speech-to-text and the on-screen graphics ("Goldman Sachs On India", "OVERWEIGHT BANKS"); live blogs;
  wires (Reuters, Bloomberg-quoted stories on ET / Moneycontrol / Business Standard); the house's own published note when online.
- Mark "UNVERIFIED - TV only" until the note or a second outlet confirms the numbers.

## How the desk reads it (the routine)
1. WHAT IS NEW: compare with the house's last stance in the thread. A CHANGE (upgrade / downgrade, new overweight) matters; a
   repeat of an old view does not. 19 Nov: industrials moved to OVERWEIGHT (was underweight Nov 2025) = the real change; banks
   were already overweight (Mar 2026).
2. MAP both sides with the link book: sectors helped (CE) and hurt (PE), today / next days / months.
3. TIMING: a strategy call is a weeks-to-months view. Intraday it adds little unless price confirms; the effect grows if other
   houses repeat it and FIIs buy.
4. CONFIRM / CANCEL with price and flows before any GO NOW: the named sectors must beat NIFTY from the whole sector, above VWAP,
   breadth widening, FII cash turning positive; cancel if NIFTY gives back half the day's gain or the favoured index falls below
   VWAP.
5. Never chase a TV recap; trade the first dip that holds VWAP on the next session, in the next-month series near expiry.

## Case 1 - Goldman Sachs on India, CNBC-TV18, Thu 19 Nov 2026, 12:44-12:46 (TV only, note not yet online)
- Said: India trailed EMs 2 years (rare in 30 years; both earlier times then +38 pp outperformance); GDP 7.9% CY26, 7.2% CY27;
  inflation 5.7% (above consensus); USD-INR ~96 in 12 months (93.28 then); results beat consensus 5 quarters, EPS revisions
  positive, Nifty EPS +15% in CY27 and CY28; AI capex peaking -> India's growth appeal returns; FII sold $64 bn, ownership at a
  decade-plus low, India has taken the largest share of EM inflows since mid-October; valuations full.
- CALL: OVERWEIGHT BANKS (rate hikes, low valuation, light positioning) and OVERWEIGHT INDUSTRIALS (strengthening growth);
  "favour domestic cyclicals".
- Desk mapping: CE - SBIN, AXISBANK, ICICIBANK, HDFCBANK, KOTAKBANK; industrials LT (leader), BEL, SIEMENS, ABB, CUMMINS,
  CGPOWER, BHEL. PE (lag, not crash) - BAJFINANCE, DLF, GODREJPROP, LODHA, ASIANPAINT, HINDUNILVR, NESTLEIND. IT mixed (outflow vs
  weaker rupee). Desk split: CE leader AXISBANK vs SBIN; PE leader BAJFINANCE vs DLF.
- Market at the time: NIFTY +~130 (~0.5%), advances 1,743 vs declines 1,583 (narrow).
- Same screens: IRDAI chairman - "seeking personal details before sharing a quote is like charging a toll for service" (insurers,
  PB Fintech: watch only, act if price confirms).
- TO FILL (server, from Kite): BANKNIFTY, NIFTY PSU BANK, capital goods, realty, the named shares at +30 min, close, 20 Nov, 26 Nov,
  19 Dec - did the call move them, and how much? This becomes the first entry of Goldman's hit rate.
