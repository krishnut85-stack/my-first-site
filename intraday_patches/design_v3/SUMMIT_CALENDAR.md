## How to read this file

- **Verification level.** Every fact here comes from web-search results. Page fetches were blocked in this session (DNS refused for rbi.org.in-type hosts, federalreserve.gov, 5paisa.com, newkerala.com), so full pages were not read. Check the source link before you trade on any row.
- **UNCONFIRMED** means no official or reliable date, speaker or quote was found. Nothing in this file is invented. Where no quote was found, the row says so.
- **IST conversions.** US Eastern in Dec = UTC-5, so IST = ET + 10:30. CET = UTC+1, so IST = CET + 4:30. PT = UTC-8, so IST = PT + 13:30.
- **F&O names.** Symbols are NSE tickers. The NSE F&O list changes every quarter, so check the current list. Names marked (non-F&O) are not in the F&O list but show where the move starts.
- **Decision vs opinion.** "Official" = a binding decision by a body that can make one, such as a rate, a tax, an AoN or a scheme. "Opinion" = a speech, guidance, forecast or wish-list.

## PART C - Lessons: what tends to matter vs fade

1. **A binding decision that surprises moves prices. A decision that was expected does not.** RBI's 50 bp + CRR cut (6 Jun 2025) sent Bank Nifty to a record. The expected 25 bp cut (7 Feb 2025) left indices red. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/crr-cut-surprised-mkts-rbi-may-await-data-before-next-rate-move-analysts-125060600413_1.html ; https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/rbi-rate-cut-fails-to-cheer-markets-benchmark-indices-end-in-red-125020701663_1.html
2. **Official intent with a deadline, from the top, moves sectors on day 1. The later formal decision is partly priced in.** Modi's "GST reform by Diwali" (15 Aug 2025) sent autos up 4.6-7%. On the Council decision day (4 Sep), analysts said stocks "price in most positives". Sources: https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/auto-stocks-zoom-hero-motocorp-surges-9-gst-reform-hopes-brighten-outlook ; https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/gst-rate-rejig-above-expectation-stocks-price-in-most-positives-analysts-125090400373_1.html
3. **A tax or rule that hits a business model directly gives the fastest, biggest single-stock moves.** After the Budget STT hike, BSE fell 15% intraday and Angel One 13%. Source: https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks/bse-share-price-plunges-15-percent-after-budget-2026-stt-hike
4. **A regulator's denial or "status quo" line gives a short relief bounce, not a trend.** SEBI called expiry-curb reports "speculative" and BSE bounced. Source: https://www.5paisa.com/news/sebi-denies-weekly-expiry-ban-rumours-bse-capital-market-stocks-bounce-back
5. **CEO or company guidance at results or analyst meets moves that one stock, usually 3-9%.** Examples: INFY on guidance cuts, GODREJCP after its analyst meet, COCHINSHIP after its analyst call. Sources: https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/infosys-shares-fall-4-after-q2-results-revenue-growth-guidance-cut-to-1-2 ; https://upstox.com/news/market-news/stocks/godrej-consumer-products-shares-fall-5-to-52-week-low-on-subdued-guidance-in-analyst-meet-what-s-in-focus-next/article-199732/ ; https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/cochin-shipyard-shares-fall-revenue-guidance-heres-why-554864-2026-09-11
6. **Defence DAC "approvals" give modest pops (+1-4%).** An AoN is the first procurement stage, not an order. Source: https://newsonair.gov.in/defence-acquisition-council-clears-capital-acquisition-proposals-worth-around-%E2%82%B92-12-lakh-crore/ (plus row 21 and 26 sources)
7. **Ministers' speeches at summits with no policy tool attached showed no reported move.** Examples: Goyal at Startup Mahakumbh, Gadkari at SIAM, Malhotra at Kautilya, Semicon 2.0 targets. Sources: rows 2, 24, 28, 29.
8. **Trade-deal talk lifts small exporters on hope. Only the actual deal moved the index** (Sensex +5% intraday on 3 Feb 2026). "Near the finish line" lines have been repeated since mid-2025 ("99 percent" done, Jun 2026) and still no Phase One signing. Sources: https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/usindia-trade-deal-lifts-shrimp-and-textile-stocks-up-to-20 ; https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/economy/india-hails-progress-in-us-trade-talks-but-no-deal-yet-2
9. **A rate decision hits hardest when oil and FII flows push the same way.** After the 7 Oct 2026 hike, Sensex fell another 1,000 points the next day as crude crossed $104. Source: https://www.oneindia.com/business/sensex-nifty-crash-market-loses-over-10-lakh-crore-as-crude-tops-104-rbi-tightens-policy-8228533.html
10. **Industry-body forecasts (NASSCOM) and pre-Budget wish-lists are context, not triggers.** No price move was reported for either. Sources: rows 17 and 37.
11. **Intraday and closing moves differ a lot**, for example BSE -15% intraday vs -8% at close (Feb 2026) and Sensex +5% vs +2.5% (Feb 2026). Price both the first 30 minutes and the close. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/markets-fm-hikes-stt-on-fo-trades-surprise-move-126020100888_1.html ; https://www.deccanherald.com/business/markets/india-us-trade-deal-drives-rally-in-stock-markets-sensex-nifty-quote-nearly-3-higher-3884564
12. **Words like "considering", "examining" or "calibrated steps" from a regulator** (SEBI, Mar 2026) produced no rule within 8 months. Treat them as a risk flag, not a trade. Source: https://newsable.asianetnews.com/amp/business/sebi-targets-fo-risks-plans-calibrated-steps-for-retail-investors-articleshow-ngbqa1r
