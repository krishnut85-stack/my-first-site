## 1. WHY ACs AND COPPER ARE LINKED
- An AC is mostly metal. The compressor has a copper motor. The condenser and evaporator coils are copper tubes with aluminium fins. There is also copper piping and wiring. *Estimate:* copper is about 15-20% of a room AC's factory cost, aluminium about 5-8%, and all raw material about 70-75%.
- Brands already raised prices: 5-15% in summer 2026, then Voltas 5-7%, Blue Star 5-8% and Havells about 5% from 1 Oct, all because of copper. Now copper is falling while those higher prices stay. That is pure margin gain.
- Rough maths: copper about 10% below its 2026 average cuts AC cost by about 1.5-2%. With price held, gross margin goes up about 150-200 bps. On a thin 5-10% EBIT margin, that can lift EPS by 15-25%.
- Time lag: brands hold 60-90 days of inventory and buy metal on monthly or quarterly prices. So the margin gain shows in Q4 FY27 (Jan-Mar) and the summer quarter, Q1 FY28.
- Why the market moves the same day: copper was the main bear story for durables all of 2026. Analysts re-price margins at spot metal prices. Shorts cover. High P/E means a small margin change gives a big price move.

## 2. THE MAP (+ gains, - loses; strength out of 3)
**GAINS (copper is a cost)**
- +++ VOLTAS, BLUESTARCO: pure room-AC players, highest copper share, just raised prices. Today's leaders.
- ++ CROMPTON (fans and pumps have copper motors), WHIRLPOOL (fridge compressors; check if it is in F&O), Lloyd (owned by HAVELLS).
- ++ (sentiment) AMBER, PGEL: AC parts makers. Their contracts with brands pass copper costs through, so the real margin effect is small. They still trade as AC proxies.
- ++ Autos: MARUTI (today's leader), M&M, TMPV, TVSMOTOR, HEROMOTOCO, EICHERMOT, BAJAJ-AUTO. A car has about 20-25 kg of copper and an EV about 80 kg. Aluminium matters more for 2-wheelers (castings, wheels). The fall in aluminium plus copper eases a 2026 cost squeeze. NIFTY AUTO had been down about 15% in 2026 on commodity prices.
- + Auto parts: MOTHERSON, UNOMINDA (wiring harnesses, but costs mostly passed to carmakers), SONACOMS (copper-heavy EV motors). Not F&O: SUBROS, Banco (radiators).
- + / 0 Power equipment: CGPOWER (motors, transformers; copper is a big cost). ABB, SIEMENS, BHEL: small effect, because price-variation clauses in their contracts pass metal costs to buyers.
- 0 DIXON: electronics assembler, metal is a small share of cost and costs pass through. Weak link, don't trade it on copper.

**LOSES (copper or aluminium is the product)**
- --- HINDCOPPER: pure copper miner, revenue moves with LME. Strongest PE.
- -- NATIONALUM: pure aluminium. VEDL: aluminium and zinc matter more than copper. HINDALCO: its copper unit earns a processing fee, so the real hit is aluminium in India. Novelis is cushioned.
- - Cables: POLYCAB, KEI (Not F&O: RRKABEL, FINCABLES, check F&O status). Metal is about 60-70% of their raw material. Selling prices follow copper down with a 2-6 week lag. Stock bought at high copper prices then gives inventory losses. Dealers wait for price cuts, so volumes dip for a few weeks. Over 1-2 quarters, lower copper actually helps demand and frees working capital. **Short-term negative, long-term neutral.** New entrants (UltraTech, Adani) are a bigger overhang than copper.

**MIXED: HAVELLS**
About a third of revenue is cables and wires, about 15-20% is Lloyd ACs, plus fans and appliances. In the first days the cable side wins: it fell 2.9% on 18 Nov. Over the quarter the appliance side wins. ICICI Securities found copper price changes and Havells' gross margin move in opposite directions (correlation -0.63). Don't trade it intraday as a copper play.
