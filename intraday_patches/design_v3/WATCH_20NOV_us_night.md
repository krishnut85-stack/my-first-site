   prices paid up, jobless claims 203k vs 197k.

3. The IPO as "sell the news" plus new supply: the timing is proven, but funds switching from other AI shares into ANT is commentary
   only, with no flow data. The macro strategist puts about 70% on rates; the AI analyst splits it about 50/50.
