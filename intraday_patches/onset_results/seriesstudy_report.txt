  HONEST CAVEATS - read before using any of this:
  - NO OPTION HISTORY: the store has no option prices. The naked option and the debit spread are PROXIES priced from the share's own
    20-day realised volatility (premium ~ 0.4 x vol x sqrt(time left)); real premiums, bid-ask (4 legs for a spread) and lot sizes differ.
  - IV CHANGES ARE NOT SEEN: the proxy holds volatility fixed. IV falling after results / events, or rising in a sell-off, changes real
    option prices in ways this study cannot measure - even a PASS is optimistic.
  - SURVIVORSHIP: only today's 218 F&O shares are in the store. Shares that left F&O, merged or collapsed in these years are missing,
    so every number here (baselines included) looks better than a trader would have got.
  - The day-k OI is NSE's end-of-day number (summed over expiries); at 15:20 Kite's live OI is close but not the same. Rollover and
    build-up are proxies from summed OI. Results / ex-dates are not filtered (no event calendar in the store). No costs or slippage.
  - 93 tries per side: a pass also needs the lift bound widened for that many tries (the guard), the held-out months and the half-years -
    a set that fails any of them is treated as luck.

6 HOW IT WAS MEASURED
  THE SERIES: onsetstudy.expiry_flags on NIFTY's market days (last Thursday of the month to Aug 2025, last Tuesday from Sep 2025, a holiday
    moves the expiry to the market day before). A series runs from the day after one expiry (day 1) to the next expiry. Only series with
    both expiries in the store are studied. The check compares each expiry with the next day's change in the summed futures OI of every
    symbol present on both days: expiring contracts vanish, so the summed OI should drop then more than on any other day of the month.
  ENTRY / EXIT: the close of day 1, 2 or 3 (a share needs a full session that day - onsetstudy.prep's rules: previous day present, no
    split jump, listed in NSE's F&O file). EXIT (desk decision): the close 4 MARKET days before the expiry day, Thursday and Tuesday
    expiries alike, holidays not counted (Tue 29 Dec 2026 -> Tue 22 Dec). A trade with an unadjusted split / bonus inside is dropped.
    A second check: an NSE trading day after a flagged expiry and on or before the scheduled last Thursday / Tuesday, or NIFTY's next
    day differing from NSE's, means a missing NIFTY minute day moved the calendar - the series ending and starting there are dropped.
  SIGNALS at the close of the entry day, from that day and earlier only. Prices on a log scale so the PE mirror is exact. Build-up: the
    price since the expiry close (day 0) and the share's OI since the DAY-1 close against the median of all F&O shares over the same
    days - the expiry drop (day 0 -> day 1) is left out because it is the rollover's information; so day 1 has no build-up signal.
    OI unit changes (NSE's OI is not split-adjusted: OI 1.8x up / down on a day the price moved < 20%, or a split jump) switch that
    share's OI signals off from that day to the end of the series (never before it: no look-ahead) and keep that series' rollover
    out of later series' history. Rollover proxy: OI on day 1 / OI the day before the
    expiry, against the share's own last 6 series, split into thirds across the shares of that series. Sector: median move of its shares
    over the previous series (expiry to expiry; a share with a split inside it is left out), ranked; top 3 of 9+ sectors (top third when fewer); breadth = share of members up.
    LOOSE sectors.json buckets ('other', 'misc', ...) get no sector signals. RSI(14) Wilder on daily closes; 20 / 50-day averages
    (geometric); NIFTY against its 50-day average. No signals within 60 rows after a split jump (the averages would mix units).
  OUTCOMES: move = (exit / entry - 1) for CE, (1 - exit / entry) for PE. MAE = the worst daily low (CE) / high (PE) after entry up to the
    exit, against the entry. Naked option = (0.5 x move - (P0 - P1)) / P0, floored at -100%%: P0 = 0.4 x vol x sqrt(days to expiry / 252)
    at entry, P1 the same at the exit, vol = 20-day realised volatility (at least 10% a year). Debit spread = a debit of 0.55 x P0 and a
    width of 1.8 x the debit, valued at its intrinsic value at the exit: result between -100% and +80% of the debit (the time value
    left 1-3 days before expiry is ignored - slightly harsh on losers). HIT = the spread result above 0. Equal money per trade.
  BASELINES: ALL = every share the same side and day; TOP = the shares of the previous series' top sector (PE: bottom). RANDOM = for every
    trade a random share of the same series, entry day and side; its hit count over 10,000 draws is drawn as the exactly equivalent
    binomial count per series. LIFT = the set's hits / the random picks' expected hits; its 95% interval resamples whole months (series)
    2,000 times (onsetstudy.boot). p = the share of the 10,000 random draws that hit as often as the set. MKT alone takes every share
    of a series or none, so against a same-series random pick its lift is exactly 1.00 by construction - it counts only in pairs.
  THE GUARD (added to the design's PASS rule): the lift's lower bound with the interval widened for the number of tries on that side
    (Bonferroni on the log lift, the month-bootstrap's standard error): with ~93 tries a plain 95% interval lets pure noise pass.
  TUNING / HELD-OUT: held-out = series starting in the last 6 months of the store (onsetstudy's cut); tuning = series that end before
    it; a series spanning the cut is used in neither. Half-years count only with 3+ series; a set needs 3+ trades in one to hold there.
