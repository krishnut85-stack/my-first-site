## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **Which group moved, from the whole basket?** Capital goods, defence, rail, cement and realty split often (20 Nov: realty at a record,
   defence flat, power equipment down). Name the group, never "infra" as one block.
2. **Why?** An order or DAC approval (OFFICIAL filing?), a policy number (budget, ministry ask - is it new or a repeat?), rates, housing,
   input costs, a broker change, global AI / data-centre capex (power equipment), retail momentum. Every FACT needs an evidence id.
3. **Where does the money go, who gains, who loses?** Capex up: LT (cleanest), BHEL (most %), then cement weeks later. Rates down: realty,
   then cement. Defence theme: BEL for options, BDL / MAZDOCK for %. Losers: the theme's laggards, the share with an order cancelled,
   power equipment on AI-capex selling. Winners AND losers, or "none found + reason".
4. **Analogy with counts:** May 2025 Sindoor run, 1 Feb 2025 capex miss, 6 Jun 2025 cut (DLF +6%, GODREJPROP about +7%), 11 Dec 2024
   cement price hike. Quote counts; one case is a story.
5. **New or priced in?** "Defence capex up 20%" was the Nov 2025 figure (FY27); the latest ask on 9 Oct 2026 was 35% - so the 19 Nov TV
   line was NOT new. Sector up 5%+ in 5 sessions = PRICED IN; count EXTREME names.
6. **Both sides** in `against`: theme fatigue, retail froth, valuations (defence at 50-70x earnings), rates rising for realty.
7. **Horizon:** order news same day; policy same day; rates policy day; cost links weeks. The trigger is the group holding VWAP and
   beating NIFTY after 09:20 with about 70% of the group green (the desk's defence rule of 20 Nov).
