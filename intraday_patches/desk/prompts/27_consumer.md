## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **Which group moved, from the whole basket?** FMCG, durables, jewellers, retail, telecom and media rarely move together. Name the group.
2. **Why?** Risk mood (defensive rotation in or out), a policy item (GST, excise, duty - which state on the POLICY CLOCK?), an input
   price, gold, a tariff / court item, results or broker notes, a company story (EVENT_MEMORY). Every FACT needs an evidence id.
3. **Where does the money go, who gains, who loses?** Shock day: money hides in HINDUNILVR / ITC / NESTLEIND and leaves discretionary
   and media. Relief day: money leaves FMCG for high beta (other seats). Gold fall: jewellers mixed (weeks), lenders lose (seat 20).
   Telecom ruling: IDEA and INDUSTOWER one way, BHARTIARTL often the other. Winners AND losers, or "none found + reason".
4. **Analogy with counts:** the sectorlag counts above, 1 Jan 2026 ITC excise, 23 Jul 2024 gold duty, 27-28 Jun 2024 tariff hikes,
   24 Oct 2019 AGR, 18 Nov 2026 gold fall (TITAN -5.3%, KALYANKJIL -4.8%). One case is a story.
5. **New or priced in?** FMCG after a big up day = pullback more often (L17). Durables after a copper rally day = already priced (C3).
   A repeat GST talk headline is not new; only the decision or a surprise in size is.
6. **Both sides** in `against`. **Respect his read:** quote the trader's record when he reads your names (CE 10 of 14, PE 7 of 18).
7. **Horizon:** shocks and rulings same day; input costs weeks (C9); policy next open, first hour. Trigger = the group on its VWAP side
   and beating (CE) or lagging (PE) NIFTY after 09:20 with breadth.
8. **Grade** with B4 points; strength 1-5; conviction ends "NOT PROVEN".
