## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **What moved, from the whole basket?** Four-wheelers vs two-wheelers vs trucks vs parts. One heavyweight carrying the index is a
   TRAP. Subtract NIFTY.
2. **Why? Rank the drivers:** crude, rates, GST / policy, sales and VAHAN, a results read-across (MARUTI -> parts), short covering,
   metals (slow). CHAIN_OF_EVENTS A: metals alone rarely give a heavyweight a big day - two or three chains pointing the same way do.
3. **Chain to names, both sides.** Leader by driver: crude -> ASHOKLEY (diesel / freight); GST -> M&M led 2025; small-car policy ->
   MARUTI; sales -> whoever surprises most. Losers: the sales misser, autos on a crude spike, TMPV on China. Fill winners AND losers,
   or "none found + reason" (on a metals-cost day the loser is in seat 24's sector - say so).
4. **Analogy with counts:** the sectorlag counts above, 5 Nov 2026 (Brent about -9%: ASHOKLEY about +9-10%, MARUTI about +4.7%), 18 -> 19
   Nov 2026, GST 2025, 1 Oct 2026 sales. Quote counts; one case is a story.
5. **New or priced in?** Autos up 5%+ in 5 sessions = PRICED IN (C4). Count EXTREME names. A user-side rally already done (19 Nov) is not
   tomorrow's edge (C3).
6. **Both sides** in `against`: the slow cost link vs a risk-off open; profit-taking after a big day (L17).
7. **Horizon:** crude, GST, sales act same day / next open; metal costs act over quarters. The trigger is the auto basket holding VWAP,
   beating NIFTY after 09:20 with breadth; a gap that took the move is "GAP TOOK it" - first dip to VWAP only.
8. **Grade** with B4 points (size, drivers aligned, short-heavy futures, winners not yet priced); strength 1-5; ends "NOT PROVEN".
