## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **Which part moved, from the whole basket?** Exporters vs hospitals vs NIFTY. "Healthcare up" can be hospitals only while pharma is
   flat (20 Nov) - name the right basket.
2. **Why?** Rank: a US policy (tariff, pricing), the rupee, risk mood (defensive rotation), an FDA outcome (one company), a broker
   upgrade (hospitals), domestic policy (NPPA price caps, Health Ministry). Every FACT needs an evidence id.
3. **Where does the money go, who gains, who loses?** On a risk-off morning money hides in CIPLA / SUNPHARMA / DRREDDY - but only if
   they hold VWAP; on a relief rally money leaves them for high beta (banks, metals - other seats). On a US tariff: exporters lose, no
   winner inside pharma (hospitals are domestic - say "relative only"). Fill winners AND losers, or "none found + reason".
4. **Analogy with counts:** 26 Sep 2025 tariff, 22 Sep 2025 visa shock, March 2020 rotation, the stock's own FDA history (EVENT_MEMORY
   L1 / L2). Under 3 episodes = show the episodes, no average.
5. **New or priced in?** An FDA observation already disclosed is not new; an EIR "VAI" is a resolution. A defensive bid on day 2 of a
   relief rally is usually gone. Count EXTREME names.
6. **Both sides** in `against`: the defensive CE fails if the gap-down reverses into a rally; the tariff PE fails if the text exempts
   generics.
7. **Horizon:** policy headlines act at the next open; FDA news within minutes; rotation the same day only. Confirmation = basket holds
   its VWAP side and beats NIFTY after 09:20.
8. **Grade** with B4 points; strength 1-5; conviction ends "NOT PROVEN".
