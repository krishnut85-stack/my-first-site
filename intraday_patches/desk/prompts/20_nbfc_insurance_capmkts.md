## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **Which group moved, from the whole basket?** NBFC vs bank basket vs NIFTY; gold lenders vs gold; brokers vs turnover. One share
   is not a group (C16): a regulator order on one company is that share only.
2. **Why?** Rank: rates / yields, liquidity, a regulator text (state: consultation / final / notified), a credit scare, gold, turnover,
   auto volumes, a results read-across. Every FACT needs an evidence id; the policy state comes from the POLICY CLOCK, never guessed.
3. **Chain to names, both sides.** A hike: losers NBFC / housing finance, winners none in your groups - say where the money goes
   (deposit-rich banks over weeks, seat 19). A gold fall: losers gold lenders, MCX only if volumes fade; winners jewellers (seat 27,
   weeks, weak). A SEBI curb: brokers and exchanges PE; a relief: the same CE plus AMCs. Winners AND losers, or "none found + reason".
4. **Analogy with counts.** EVENT_MEMORY / LINK_BOOK cases by id: 16-17 Nov 2023 risk weights (SBICARD -5 to -7%), 6 Jun 2025 surprise
   cut (BAJFINANCE +4.9%), 1 Feb 2026 STT (BSE -8%, bounce next day), 18 Nov 2026 gold fall (MANAPPURAM -4.59%, MUTHOOTFIN -3.97%).
   Quote the episode count; under 3 episodes = show episodes only, no average (EVENT_MEMORY D).
5. **New or priced in?** A repeat headline (a draft rule re-reported) is REPEAT, NO NEW FACT - expect a fade, no BUY NOW. A gap that
   already took the move is "GAP TOOK it" with a pullback trigger. Sector up 5%+ in 5 sessions = PRICED IN.
6. **Both sides.** The case against in `against`: e.g. NBFCs lagging banks may catch up (L16) or may lag because rates are rising.
7. **Horizon.** Regulator shocks: minutes to day 1, bounce day 2. Rate moves: policy day after 10:15. Gold: the gap at the open, then
   the trend only if gold keeps falling. Confirmation = the basket holds its VWAP side and beats NIFTY after 09:20.
8. **Grade** with B4 points; strength 1-5; conviction ends "NOT PROVEN".
