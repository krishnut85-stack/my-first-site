## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **What moved, from the whole basket?** Steel vs non-ferrous vs mining vs NIFTY; breadth; did it close near its extreme (persistence)?
   One name down on its own news (a target cut, an OFS) is not the sector.
2. **Why?** Take seats 7 and 3's cause (China demand, dollar, LME, supply), then add the Indian layer: duties, OFS / blocks, broker
   calls, results. Name the cause per share - the same day can have VEDL down on a broker note and HINDALCO up on its own.
3. **Where does the money go, who gains, who loses?** Producers on the metal's side; on a commodity slump the users gain slowly (seat 23,
   26, 27 - weeks); on risk-off nobody in the chain gains - say "none found: risk-off, users fall too" and point to defensives (seats 22,
   27). Fill winners AND losers, leader first.
4. **Analogy with counts:** B3 / B4 / B7 cases by id (24 Sep 2024 stimulus, 7 Apr 2025 tariffs: Nifty Metal -6.75 to -8%; 11 Apr 2025
   pause: Metal +4.09%; 23 May 2022 duty) and the sectorlag counts. One case is a story.
5. **New or priced in?** Overnight moves confirmed by the LME close (22:30) or SHFE (06:30) are the only next-day edge (C3); a second day
   of the same fall is smaller. A repeat duty headline is not new. Count EXTREME / oversold names.
6. **Both sides** in `against`: a gap-down that recovers (do not chase), a short-covering spike in short-heavy names.
7. **Horizon:** producer moves same day, mostly in the first 60-90 minutes; users weeks. The PE trigger is the basket below VWAP and
   weaker than NIFTY after 09:20 with breadth; a recovery back above VWAP cancels it.
8. **Grade** with B4 points (size vs normal, linked metals together, persistence, short build-up, overnight confirmation); strength
   1-5; ends "NOT PROVEN".
