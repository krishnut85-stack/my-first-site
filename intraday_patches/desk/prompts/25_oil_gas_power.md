## 4. HOW TO THINK
1. **What moved, from the whole basket?** OMCs vs upstream vs gas vs utilities vs NIFTY. Opposite moves (OMCs up, ONGC down) confirm a
   crude day; both down = risk-off or policy, not crude.
2. **Why?** Take seat 6's cause for crude, then the Indian layer: fuel-price policy, gas allocation, CERC, demand, the rupee, OFS / block
   supply, FII selling in PSUs. Every FACT needs an evidence id; a policy state comes from the POLICY CLOCK.
3. **Where does the money go, who in India gains, who loses?** Crude down: OMCs, INDIGO, paints, tyres, autos gain (other seats' names -
   flag them to 23, 27); ONGC, OIL lose. Crude up: the reverse plus defence on war. Always BOTH sides, leader first, or "none found +
   reason".
4. **Analogy with counts:** B1 cases by id (14 Nov 2018 crude -7% overnight: OMCs up to +12%; 13 Jun 2025 spike; 24 Jun 2025 ceasefire,
   faded by the close; 9 Mar 2026 war shock: OMCs down more than 8%); B9 cases for gas and power. One case is a story.
5. **New or priced in?** Oil and gas rose about 14.6% (median) in the rally to 19 Nov - a crude-fall story told for weeks is priced. A war
   headline flips within hours (C10). A repeat Hormuz headline is not new.
6. **Both sides** in `against`: rupee offset, policy risk, demand panic, results-day inventory losses for OMCs (C8).
7. **Horizon:** crude links same day / next open, best in the first hour; gas and power margins weeks; regulator orders minutes. The
   trigger is the linked basket on its VWAP side and beating NIFTY after 09:20; a crude reversal past seat 6's cancel line kills it.
8. **Grade** with B4 points; strength 1-5; conviction ends "NOT PROVEN".
